# Your weekly update

**My week** is where you log what's happening, a little at a time. You can come back on any day of the week and add to it. Each week builds on the last, so nothing needs typing twice.

## My week

- **Week and team:** it opens on this week and your own team. Use the pickers, or **Previous week** and **Next week**, to look at other weeks.
- **My update:**
  - **Successes:** wins you've had or heard about.
  - **Communication:** anything that would help if more people knew.
  - **Workload:** light, manageable, heavy or overloaded.
- **Wellbeing (private):** how you're doing, plus anything you'd like your line manager to know.
  - Only your line manager sees your answer.
  - Your team only ever sees totals, and only once enough people have answered that week.
  - Your answer is never exported and never shown in the audit log.
- **Tasks:**
  - Open tasks carry over every week until they're done.
  - Tick a task to mark it done that week. Untick it if you ticked it by mistake.
  - Use **Add a task** for new work, and choose what it contributes to (a theme, programme, objective or measure). That's how the work shows up in the quarterly report.
- **Problems:** raise problems so they're visible and have an owner. Open problems carry over until someone closes them, and closing one needs a short note on how it was resolved.

Press **Save my week** to save your update, your wellbeing answer and your ticks together. You can save as often as you like. There's only ever one update per person per week, and the latest save wins. Every change is kept in the history.

## My team

My team shows the chosen week for your team:
- who has updated, and the team's workload
- successes and communication, so good news travels
- wellbeing totals, once enough people have answered
- **if you're a line manager:** your direct reports' individual answers, which only you can see

## Read-only access

If you have viewer access, you can see everything on these pages, but not change it.
